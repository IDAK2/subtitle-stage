# Frontend Design Contract

## screenArchitecture
proscenium-workspace

## aboveTheFold
The source cue is projected over an empty stage. A cue lamp, hanging translation rail, and recessed orchestra pit are visible without a marketing introduction.

## primaryInteraction
Load a show, rehearse an adaptation for the illuminated cue, and submit it to the ensemble jury.

## layoutMap
Fly tower at left, proscenium stage in the center, cue desk at right, subtitle rail above the orchestra pit, and transaction callboard at the bottom.

## visualMetaphor
Live subtitle rehearsal.

## motionSystem
Curtain Rise, Cue Lamp Snap, Subtitle Glide, Blackout Strike, and Curtain Call Bow.

## effectStack
CSS curtain folds and an animated spotlight cone.

## componentShapeLanguage
Curtain folds, cue lamps, hanging subtitle strips, stage tape marks, and fly-system ropes.

## customVisualComponents
ProsceniumStage, CueLamp, SubtitleRail, OrchestraPit, FlyTower, and Callboard.

## bannedFromThisBuild
No generic centered hero, equal feature cards, stats strip, chip row, submit-feed skeleton, three-column footer, dark navy plus purple gradient, hinged paper leaves, or gallery plinths.

## proofOfDifference
This is a deep stage with source and adaptation occupying separate theatrical planes. It does not reuse the archive gallery or tea fan's spatial composition, gestures, result display, palette, or motion.
